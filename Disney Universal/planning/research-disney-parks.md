## 1. Park ranking + days

**1) Magic Kingdom (2 days), 2) Hollywood Studios (1), 3) Animal Kingdom (1), 4) EPCOT (1 — the skippable one).**

- **MK — the only 2-day park.** Musts: Mine Train, Big Thunder, Pirates, Haunted Mansion, Jungle Cruise, Peter Pan, Buzz, Pooh, Dumbo+Barnstormer, PeopleMover; Space Mtn at 44"+, TRON at 48"+, Tiana's at 40"+ if brave. Caveat: Frontierland heavily walled (Cars/Piston Peak construction); party nights close at 6 p.m.
- **HS — strong #2 in 2026.** Slinky (38"), Toy Story Mania (no height), Saucers, **Rise (40")**, Smugglers Run w/ Grogu mission, Runaway Railway (no height), Frozen Sing-Along. Construction-heavy (Monstropolis, Animation Courtyard). Fantasmic may be too intense/late.
- **AK — 1 day, easily half.** Safari (morning), Na'vi River, Festival of the Lion King, **Zootopia: Better Zoogether!** (new Nov 2025) + Judy/Nick meet. DINOSAUR/DinoLand permanently closed Feb 2026 (Tropical Americas construction). At 44"+: FoP + Everest.
- **EPCOT — cut first if shortening.** Frozen + Remy (no height), Test Track 40", Soarin' 40", Guardians 42". Great as a half-rides/half-wander recovery day with Luminous; weakest kid-dense hours.

Allocations — 5 days: MK 2, HS 1, AK 1, EP 1. 4 days: MK 2, HS 1, AK 1. 3 days: MK 1–2, HS 1, AK 1.

## 2. Lightning Lane 2026

- **Multi Pass** — pre-book 3 timed returns, rolling rebook after each use; tiered (one Tier-1). **EPCOT now tiered in 2026** (Frozen/Remy/Test Track — pick one). Prices/person/day: MK $25–45, EP $20–40, HS $20–38, AK $15–30. Prices appear ~21 days out. ([Park Autopilot](https://parkautopilot.com/blog/lightning-lane-changes-2026), [Thrill Data live LL tracker](https://www.thrill-data.com/lightning-lane-prices))
- **Single Pass** — à la carte for the 5 excluded headliners: Mine Train, TRON, Guardians, **Rise**, FoP. ~$17–25/person/ride; max 2/day.
- **Premier Pass** — MK $329–449/person/day etc. Not for a family of 3+.
- **Booking windows: resort guests 7 days ahead (7 a.m. ET, whole stay); off-site 3 days.** Resort guests clear the good slots first — strong on-site argument, plus 30-min Early Entry daily (confirmed through 2027).

**Recommendation:** MK = Multi Pass yes (Pan/Tiana's + Big Thunder + Jungle Cruise; Mine Train via rope drop, Single Pass as fallback). HS = Multi Pass near-mandatory (Slinky + TSM + Railway) + **Rise Single Pass ~$24 — the most justified buy**. AK = usually no (rope-drop safari + Na'vi); FoP Single Pass ~$17 if he's 44"+. EPCOT = optional; Guardians mostly standby in 2026 (VQ largely retired — verify in app at 7 a.m.).

## 3. Rope drop, rhythm, evening shows

- On-site Early Entry 30 min daily; arrive at tapstiles 45–60 min before official open. Off-site: 45 min early.
- **MK:** Mine Train first → Pan/Pooh → Tiana's/Big Thunder by 10:30. **HS:** Slinky first → Saucers/TSM; Rise via Single Pass mid-morning. **AK:** FoP first if 44"+, else Safari first → Na'vi. **EPCOT:** Frozen (main gate) or Remy (International Gateway) first.
- **Mid-day break non-negotiable:** park 8–1, pool/nap 1–5, return 5–show. This is why MK gets 2 days and why staying monorail/Skyliner-close matters. Feed the kid by 9:30; 2–3 rope-drop targets max.
- **Shows:** **Happily Ever After — the one worth keeping him up for** (10 p.m.; 9:30 Aug 31–Sep 21; never on party nights). **Disney Starlight parade** (~8:15 run ideal) + fireworks = the big night. Fantasmic 8:30 doable but villain-heavy. Luminous = most skippable.

## 4. State of the parks, Aug 2026

- **MK:** Rivers of America/Tom Sawyer Island gone — Cars "Piston Peak" land under construction (~2027–28); Frontierland walls. Big Thunder reopened May 2026 (38"). Carousel of Progress closed through ~2027. **MNSSHP Aug 7–Oct 31 2026 (39 nights): MK closes 6 p.m. to day guests, no fireworks/parade those nights; tickets $119–229.** Very Merry: Nov 8–Dec 22, $189–229, same pattern. **Build MK days around non-party nights** — or lean in: a party ticket (enter 4 p.m., low waits, trick-or-treat, exclusive parade/fireworks) is genuinely great for a 6yo, expect to leave by 10–11.
- **EPCOT:** Test Track v3 reopened Jul 2025. LL tiering new for 2026.
- **HS:** Muppet*Vision closed → Monstropolis (~2028). RnRC now Muppets Electric Mayhem (May 2026). Animation Courtyard walled. Occasional early-close event nights — check calendar.
- **AK:** DinoLand/DINOSAUR gone (Tropical Americas: Encanto + Indiana Jones, 2027). Zootopia show new. Rafiki's Planet Watch → Bluey experience "summer 2026" — **status unconfirmed, check before promising.**

## 5. Characters & dining

- **ADRs open 60 days out ~5:45–6 a.m. ET** (on-site guests book whole stay at their mark).
- Free meets he'll love: Buzz (HS), safari Mickey (AK), Judy & Nick (AK), Chewbacca/BB-8 (HS — six new meets added 2026), Mickey at Town Square (MK).
- **Character meals ranked:** 1) Chef Mickey's (Contemporary — the classic 6yo pick, no ticket needed) 2) Tusker House (pairs with AK morning) 3) Garden Grill (fixes EPCOT's character gap) 4) Story Book Dining at Artist Point (Evil Queen — hardest ADR, book at exactly 60) 5) Hoop-Dee-Doo (most reliably kid-delighting dinner show).
- **Skippable:** Cinderella's Royal Table, Akershus (princess-focused), Be Our Guest (no characters), Space 220. Rule: quick service + ~1 character meal per 2 days; over-ADRing kills flexibility.

## 6. Rider Switch (free, every gated ride)

Whole party to the entrance with the child → Group 1 rides → Group 2 (adult + kid) gets digital pass → enters via Lightning Lane, no second wait. **If Group 2 is one adult, one Group-1 member may ride AGAIN with them.** With LL: everyone riding needs their own valid LL (no laundering). Use at: FoP, Everest, Space Mtn, TRON, RnRC, Guardians if under 42". Extra adults + Rider Switch = every headliner covered without leaving the kid.
