## Best 7-day windows (last 12 months — use as proxy for the same weeks next year)

1. **Late August** (Aug 22–30): ~26 blended — school just started, hurricane-season gamble, hot
2. **Early September** (Sep 1–8, post-Labor Day): ~26–27
3. **Late Sep → early Oct** (Sep 25–Oct 5): ~26–27 — slightly cooler than Aug, still hurricane season
4. **Mid-November pre-Thanksgiving** (Nov 16–22): ~27 — cool, low rain, Christmas decor already up
5. **Early-mid May** (May 6–13) and **mid-June** (Jun 11–18): ~27 — surprisingly good in 2026

Worst: Christmas–New Year's week runs 54–56 blended — literally double the best weeks.

## Day-of-week (avg wait, recent era)

- **Magic Kingdom: Sunday is the quietest** (21.6), Friday next; Saturday worst.
- **Hollywood Studios & Animal Kingdom: Wednesday best**, weekend worst (AK Sat 37.6).
- **USF/IOA: Wednesday best** (22.7/26.1), Saturday clearly worst (31.9/36.5).
- **Epic Universe barely varies by day** (50.9–55.9, Sunday lowest) — it's capacity-constrained every day; the win there is early entry + rope drop, not day choice.

## Epic Universe trend

Opening frenzy is cooling: 62.9 avg (Jun 2025) → 46–54 range (spring/summer 2026). Still runs ~2× the other six parks. Expect further softening into 2027, but plan Epic as the highest-wait day of the trip regardless.

## Working conclusions (pending research-agent findings)

- Target **early September** or **mid-November pre-Thanksgiving**; late Sep–early Oct as alternate. All require pulling the 6-year-old from school for some days.
- Sequence parks: **Epic on the day early entry is available** (hotel-dependent), MK on Sunday, HS/AK midweek, USF/IOA (if included) on Wednesday.
- Sep/Oct caveat: MK closes early many nights for Halloween party (verify calendar); hurricane risk is real but refundable-rate planning covers it.

## Late-January deep dive (added after dates chosen: Jan 23–30, 2027)

Day-by-day analog week (Jan 17–Feb 1, 2026, avg wait minutes):

- **Disney weekdays are the year's emptiest**: MK 15–28 (Tue Jan 27 hit **15**), HS 34–45, AK 20–38, EP 25–42. Weekend bump is modest.
- **Epic INVERTS in January**: Sat/Sun 49–59 vs Mon–Thu 65–76. January was Epic's worst month of 2026 (66 avg). → **Epic on Sunday**, with hotel early entry; Express Pass Now as contingency.
- **USF/IOA**: fine Mon–Thu (USF 17–31, IOA 26–43); Saturday spikes (IOA 53–67).
- 2025 showed the same shape (pre-Epic): quiet weekdays, moderate weekends, AK weekend spikes.

Conclusion baked into MASTER-PLAN v2: Universal first (Epic Sun, classics Mon), Disney Tue–Fri.
