# Research: trip structure, hotels, tickets, timing (2026)

*Deep-research output, August 2026. All prices 2026 figures; both resorts use date-based pricing.*

## 1. Trip structure

- **7–8 nights** for 4 Disney park days + 1–2 Universal days; 6–7 nights if trimming to 3 Disney days or dropping the rest day. Best family pattern: 2 park days → **pool/rest day** → 2 park days → transfer → Epic. ([FunStayHomes](https://funstayhomes.com/split-stay-disney-world-and-universal-in-one-trip/), [KidsParkGuide](https://kidsparkguide.com/how-many-days-disney-world-kids/))
- **Split stay wins**: hotel perks are the whole game (Universal = 1-hr Epic early entry; Disney = 30-min early entry + 7-day LL window) and you can't get both from one base. Resorts are ~9 mi apart — one ~$30–45 rideshare. ([WDWinfo 2026](https://www.wdwinfo.com/planning/the-great-2026-pivot-3-reasons-why-were-committing-to-a-disney-universal-split-stay/))
- **Disney first, Universal last**: do the reservation-heavy half fresh; Universal's short walks/Express make Disney feel worse if done first; ends on the "new park" high. Epic's quietest day is **Sunday** (inverted pattern vs Disney; no local AP surge yet — expected to flip when Epic APs arrive ~2027) → "Disney Mon–Fri, Epic Sunday" is an unusually strong template. ([DTB Epic crowds](https://www.disneytouristblog.com/epic-universe-lowest-crowd-days-week-best-worst/))

## 2. Universal hotels

**All 11 on-site hotels get Epic Early Park Admission** (up to 1 hr, valid check-in AND check-out days) + early entry at USF/IOA Wizarding areas + 30 min Volcano Bay. **Note: EPA land lineup has changed 3× in 2026 — re-verify near travel** (one source says Celestial+SNW+Dark Universe; another says Ministry+SNW+Berk). ([Undercover Tourist](https://www.undercovertourist.com/blog/epic-universe-early-park-admission/), [Loews](https://www.loewshotels.com/universal-orlando-resort/special-offers/early-park-admission))

| Hotel | 2026 nightly | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Helios Grand** (inside Epic) | $293–500+; summer promos ~$200 | Private gate into Epic — best for midday breaks with a 6yo; sleeps 4–5. No Express included at any tier. |
| **Stella Nova / Terra Luna** (across from Epic) | $147–300; promos ~$90 | Walk to Epic gate; splash-pad pool; rooms cap at 4 |
| **Cabana Bay** | Std $124–200; **Family Suite (sleeps 6) $172–300** | Best one-room fit if +2 adults; Epic is a bus ride |
| **Royal Pacific / Hard Rock / Portofino** | ~$200 promo–$400+ | Free Unlimited Express — **USF/IOA ONLY, not Epic** |

**Epic Express:** no hotel includes it, ever (per Universal). Separately: single-use, ~$130–330 by date. **New Apr 2026: "Express Pass Now"** — in-app single-ride skips ~$20–30/ride — the cheap surgical option. With hotel EPA + 2 Universal days, skip full Express. **Single-rider lines are NOT a family workaround** (operators won't pair a parent with a young child). ([DTB Express guide](https://www.disneytouristblog.com/express-pass-epic-universe-pros-cons-buying-guide/), [DTB Express Pass Now](https://www.disneytouristblog.com/universal-epic-universe-rolls-out-express-pass-now-single-ride-line-skipping/))

**Picks:** value → Stella Nova/Terra Luna; splurge → Helios Grand; party of 5 → Cabana Bay Family Suite.

## 3. Disney hotels

**Resort perks confirmed for 2026:** 30-min Early Entry all 4 parks daily (the "ending 2026" news is Disneyland CA only); **LL Multi Pass booking 7 days before check-in for the whole stay** vs 3 days off-site; extra-early MK entry during party season. Extended evening hours = deluxe only. ([Disney](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/resort-hotels-benefits/), [DFB](https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2026/02/06/everything-you-need-to-know-about-disney-worlds-lightning-lane-hotel-perk/))

| Resort | 2026 rate (incl. tax) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pop Century** (Value, Skyliner) | from $213 (discounts $170s–190s) | Sleeps 4; the go-to value |
| **Art of Animation suites** (Value, Skyliner) | $519–912 | Sleeps 6, 2 baths — beds fit adults |
| **Caribbean Beach** (Moderate, Skyliner hub) | $268–586 | Best moderate for young kids: pirate pool, **5th-sleeper Murphy bed** |
| **Port Orleans FQ/Riverside** | $314–533 | Bus-only; FQ compact runner-up |

**Skyliner is a real perk** (gondola to EPCOT+HS, kids love it; closes in storms, MK/AK still buses). If +2 adults: Caribbean 5th-sleeper (cheapest), AoA suite (most comfortable), or two Pop rooms (~$425–500 total). **Discounts:** "Stay Longer Save More" up to 30% off 5+ nights late Jul–early Oct 2026; fall 20–30% room discounts are the annual pattern; Free Dining predicted to return for 2027 with spring booking. ([MouseSavers](https://www.mousesavers.com/2026-pop-century-room-rates-season-dates/))

## 4. Off-site

Windsor Hills homes ~$208–400; Encore $368–440+fees. You'd lose: both early entries, 7-day LL window, free Disney transport; add $35/day Disney parking + rental car. **Verdict: on-site for this trip shape.** Off-site only pencils at 5+ people and 7–8 nights — and even then book 1–2 Universal hotel nights for the Epic morning.

## 5. Transport — no rental car

- MCO → hotels: Uber/Lyft $30–55 (+$5.80 airport fee); Mears Connect ~$95 RT for 3; Tiffany Towncar ~$150–180 RT incl. car seats + grocery stop.
- **A 6-year-old legally needs only a seat belt in FL rideshares** (booster still recommended; Uber Car Seat available ~$10 extra).
- Disney↔Universal mid-trip: UberXL ~$30–45, 20–25 min. No public shuttle.
- Rental math: ~$420–700/wk + hotel parking + gas vs ~$115–150 total rideshares → car only pencils with lots of off-site meals.

## 6. Tickets

- **Buy from authorized resellers**: Undercover Tourist (~$70 off 3+ day WDW), Get Away Today, Boardwalk Ticketing, AAA, Sam's Club. Aggregator: [MouseSavers](https://www.mousesavers.com/walt-disney-world-vacation-discounts-and-deals/walt-disney-world-ticket-discounts/).
- **Disney 4-day base: $523 (cheap season) – $773 (Christmas)** incl. tax; child only ~$5/day less. Hopper +$91–112. **4-Park Magic Ticket: 4 days from $99/day** (one park/day, summer–fall 2026).
- **Universal:** 3+ day park-to-park = unlimited Epic w/ same-day hopping; 1–2 day = one designated Epic day. **Best deal: Buy 3 Days Get 2 Free** (travel Apr 30–Dec 16 2026, buy by Sep 16) ≈ **$65/day** for all 3 parks incl. Epic every day.
- **Per-person, 4 Disney + 2 Universal days: adult ~$815–1,010; child ~$780–975.** Family of 3: ~$2,400–3,000 core; ~$2,250–2,400 with promos via resellers.

## 7. When to go (ranked)

1. **Nov 29 – Dec 13, 2026** — #1 week of the year: low crowds, full Christmas decor, driest month, 72–75°F. Caveat: MVMCP closes MK at 6 p.m. 2–3×/week — MK on non-party nights.
2. **Late Jan – early Feb 2027** (esp. Jan 31–Feb 5) — lowest crowds+prices, Festival of the Arts. Caveats: refurb season (**Peter Pan closed Jan 6–Feb 2, 2027**; Skyliner down Jan 24–30), avoid Marathon Weekend Jan 6–10 & MLK; pool may be cold.
3. **Oct 20–30, 2026** — low crowds, rain drops, low-80s, Food & Wine. Avoid Oct 9–17 surge; Wine & Dine race Oct 22–25 (hotel impact only).
4. **Late Aug – Sep 2026** — absolute lowest crowds but ~91°F, daily storms, peak hurricane season, MNSSHP 6 p.m. MK closes ~3 nights/week. High risk/reward; Disney waives change fees under hurricane warnings.
5. **Avoid:** Nov 2–10 (Jersey Week), Thanksgiving week, Dec 14–Jan 3, Presidents Day, Mar 14–Apr 5 2027, runDisney weekends (Wine & Dine Oct 22–25 2026, Marathon Jan 6–10, Princess Half Feb 25–Mar 1, Springtime Apr 15–18 2027).

## 8. Budget (family of 3, 6–7 nights, flights excluded)

- **Economy ~$5,000–6,000:** Pop (5 nts) + Stella Nova/Terra Luna (2 nts) ≈ $1,300–1,500; promo tickets ≈ $2,250–2,500; quick-service food ≈ $1,250–1,550; rideshares ~$150. No LL/Express — both early entries instead.
- **Comfort ~$8,000–10,000:** Caribbean Beach + Helios ≈ $2,300–3,000; tickets + Hopper + LL on 3 days + optional Epic Express ≈ $3,300–4,200; food w/ character meals ≈ $1,900–2,450; transport ~$200.
- +2 adults: Cabana Bay suite / AoA suite / two Pop rooms; +$815–1,010 tickets per adult.

## Bottom line

**7–8 nights, Nov 29–Dec 13 2026** (or Jan 31–Feb 6 2027 on a budget): Disney on-site first (Pop or Caribbean Beach — 2 days, rest day, 2 days, MK on a non-party night, LL booked at 7-day mark), one rideshare to Stella Nova/Terra Luna or Helios for the final 2 nights, **Epic on Sunday with early entry**, optional USF/IOA day on the 3+2 promo, fly home from there. No rental car.
