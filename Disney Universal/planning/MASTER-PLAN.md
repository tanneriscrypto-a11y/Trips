## The plan in one paragraph

Drive down Saturday **Jan 23, 2027** (~7 hrs from Newnan via I-75), do **Universal FIRST — Epic on Sunday Jan 24** — because January flips Epic's crowd pattern (weekends ~50 min avg, weekdays 65–75 in the 2026 analog week) while Disney's weekdays are the year's emptiest (MK hit a 15-min average on the analog Tuesday). Three nights at a Universal hotel (Helios Grand splurge — inside Epic, private gate, perfect for a midday break with River; Stella Nova to save ~$600), then a 30-minute drive to a Disney moderate for four nights: MK Tuesday, HS Wednesday, AK-morning + EPCOT-evening hopper Thursday, MK round-2 Friday, **drive home Saturday Jan 30**. The Skyliner is confirmed closed Jan 24–30 — irrelevant since we have the car (resort guests park free at Disney hotels AND parks). Estimated all-in for 3 people: **~$8,500** — inside budget even with the Helios splurge.

## Why Universal first / Epic on Sunday (data)

From Thrill Data day-by-day, analog week Jan 17–Feb 1, 2026:

- **Epic inverts in January**: Sat/Sun ~49–59 min average; Mon–Thu 65–76. (No local passholders yet; weekday tour groups.) January was also Epic's worst month of 2026 (66 avg) — so Sunday + hotel early entry + last-hour Battle at the Ministry are the whole strategy. "Express Pass Now" ($20–30 single-ride, in-app) is the surgical backup for Mine-Cart Madness; budget allows it.
- **Disney weekdays in late Jan are the emptiest of the year**: MK 15–28 min, HS 34–45, AK 20–35, EPCOT 25–42. No party-night closures (parties end Dec 22), fireworks every night, and January hours are shorter — which actually suits a 6-year-old (fireworks end by 9–10).
- **Classic Universal parks on Monday** (USF ~25, IOA ~31 in analog) — fine in January; avoids their Saturday spike.
